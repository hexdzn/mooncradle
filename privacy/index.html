Mooncradle privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Mooncradle is a baby log for parents and caregivers. It is made by Piyush ("we"), an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter. The short version: we never see it. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What you enter

You may enter your child's name, birthday, feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, medicines, growth measurements and notes, and in Memories, milestones and photos. You decide what to enter, and every field except the logs themselves is optional.

Where it's stored

  • On your iPhone and Apple Watch. Everything is saved on your device first, so the app works with no connection.
  • In your own iCloud, if you're signed in. Mooncradle syncs your log to your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit so it reaches your other devices. The content of each log is stored in CloudKit's encrypted fields, which Apple describes as end-to-end encrypted. We have no access to your iCloud data. You can turn this off in Mooncradle › Settings › Sync with iCloud.
  • Memory photos. Photos you add to Memories are kept on your iPhone, reduced in size, with their location and other hidden details removed. With iCloud sync on, they're stored in your own iCloud too, so they reach your other devices and anyone you share the log with. Apple encrypts them in storage and on the way, but unlike the text of your log they aren't end-to-end encrypted. We have no access to them either way.
  • With people you invite. If you share the log with a partner or caregiver, the people you invite can see and edit that family's log (every child in it) from their own iCloud account. You can stop sharing at any time in Settings › Share with partner or caregiver, and anyone you invited can leave.

What we collect

Nothing. Mooncradle has no servers of its own and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.

Reminders, Lock Screen and Watch

Feed and medicine reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. The Lock Screen timer (Live Activity) and the Apple Watch app get their information from your iPhone. None of this leaves your devices except through your own iCloud, as above.

Camera and Photos

Mooncradle uses the camera only while you're taking a memory photo. Choosing from Photos uses Apple's picker, which gives the app only the photos you pick; Mooncradle can't see the rest of your library. "Save to Photos" asks once for permission to add photos, and can't read your library either.

Import and export

When you import a file from another baby app, it's read on your iPhone and only the logs you confirm are saved. Exports (CSV and PDF reports, the memories PDF book, labelled photos, collages and growth videos) are created on your iPhone; where they go is up to you, for example when you share one with your doctor.

Children

Mooncradle is meant for adults: parents and caregivers recording information about a child. It isn't directed at children and doesn't collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete a single log by tapping it, then Delete entry. Remove a milestone or memory photo by opening it, then Remove. Remove a child (with their logs and memories) in Settings. Deleting the app removes the data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to the Settings app › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Mooncradle.

Not medical advice

Mooncradle records what you enter. It doesn't diagnose or give medical advice. Ask your doctor or health visitor about your child's health.
