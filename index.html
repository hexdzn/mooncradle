One tap

Made for half asleep.

Left, Right, Bottle, Wet, Dirty, Sleep and Pump sit on the home screen. One tap logs it, and Undo is always there.

  • The breastfeeding timer remembers which side is next.
  • “Fed 2h 10m ago” is always at the top. No math at night.
  • Night mode dims everything and shows only what matters.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

The timer, without unlocking.

A running feed lives on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Switch sides or finish the feed right from there.

Apple Watch

Log from your wrist.

Feeds, bottles and diapers in a tap, with the timer right there. Out of range of your phone? Taps wait on the Watch and land at the right time.

  • Complications show the time since the last feed.

Everything else

All of it, free.

No paywall on the features you need at 3 am.

Why free?

No catch.

Mooncradle is made by one independent developer, not a company with investors to pay back.

Your log lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, so there are no servers to run. That keeps the cost close to nothing, and it's why the app can stay free without ads or selling anything about you.

Everything in the app today is free, and it will stay that way.

Privacy

We never see your data.

Your log stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. Mooncradle's App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

No account

No ads or tracking

No analytics, no third-party code, no servers of our own.

Your iCloud
