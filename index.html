Mooncradle support

Mooncradle is a free baby log for iPhone and Apple Watch: feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, medicine and growth, shared with your partner through iCloud. No ads, no subscription, no account.

Need help or found a bug?

Email piyushggrover@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and what you tapped. You'll get a reply from a person.

Common questions

How do I share with my partner?
Settings › Share with partner or caregiver, then share the invite link. When they open it on their iPhone, you both log into the same log. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

Sync says it can't connect.
Check you're signed in to iCloud in the Settings app, and that Sync with iCloud is on in Mooncradle's Settings. Logging keeps working without a connection; everything catches up when you're back online.

How do I add a second child?
Tap your child's name at the top of the home screen, then Add a child.

Can I get my data out?
Yes. Settings › Export PDF or CSV.

Is my data private?
Yes. It stays on your devices and in your own iCloud; we never see it. Read the privacy policy.

Mooncradle records what you enter. It isn't medical advice; ask your doctor about your child's health.
